## How to Start Creating Anime Art with PixAI
If you're new to AI-generated anime art, the best approach is to keep your workflow simple. Rather than trying to master every feature on day one, focus on learning the core steps of creating and refining a character. PixAI's anime-focused interface makes this easy by organizing the tools you'll use most into a straightforward workflow.

### **1. Create an Account and Explore the Community**
After signing up, spend a few minutes browsing PixAI's homepage and community gallery. You'll find artwork from other creators, along with the models and prompts they used. Studying successful examples is one of the quickest ways to understand different anime styles and gather inspiration for your own projects.

### **2. Choose an Anime Model**
Your choice of model has a significant impact on the final image. Some models produce colorful modern anime artwork, while others are better suited for fantasy themes, softer illustrations, or highly detailed character portraits.
If you're just getting started, begin with a popular community-recommended model. Once you're comfortable, experiment with different models to find the style that best matches your creative vision.

### **3. Write a Simple Prompt**
Don't worry about creating the perfect prompt on your first attempt. Start with your character's core details, then refine the prompt over multiple generations.
A good beginner prompt usually includes:
- Character appearance
- Hair and eye color
- Clothing
- Pose
- Facial expression
- Background
- Lighting
- Overall anime style

For example:
Anime boy with messy black hair, green eyes, wearing a black hoodie and headphones, standing on a neon-lit city street at night, cinematic anime style.
Keeping prompts simple makes it easier to see how each change affects the generated image.

### **4. Learn from Community Creations**
One of PixAI's biggest strengths is its active community. Browse popular artwork to discover useful prompts, models, and settings.
Instead of copying someone else's work, treat these examples as learning resources. Understanding why a particular combination produces great results will help you develop your own style more quickly.

### **5. Add LoRAs When You Need More Control**
Once you're comfortable generating basic anime artwork, start experimenting with LoRAs.
LoRAs let you introduce specific artistic styles, clothing designs, facial features, poses, or other character traits that can be difficult to achieve through prompting alone.
A good rule for beginners is to use one LoRA at a time. This makes it easier to understand how each one influences the final image before combining multiple LoRAs in future projects.

### **6. Refine Your Character**
Your first generation is rarely your last. Small adjustments to prompts, reference images, and editing settings can dramatically improve facial expressions, proportions, poses, and overall consistency.
If you're building an original character or VTuber persona, using reference images throughout the process also helps maintain a consistent appearance across different scenes and illustrations.

### **7. Save What Works**
As you generate more artwork, keep track of the prompts, models, and LoRAs that produce your favorite results.
Building your own collection of successful combinations makes future projects faster and helps you develop a recognizable artistic style over time.
Like any creative tool, AI art rewards experimentation. Every generation teaches you something about how prompts, models, and LoRAs work together, making it easier to create better results with each new project.
